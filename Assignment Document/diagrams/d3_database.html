Database Schema — Principal Collections & Relationships
User
  • _id (PK)
  • name, email
  • passwordHash
  • role
Product
  • _id (PK)
  • name, category
  • price, gstRate
  • unit, stockQty
  • reorderLevel
Sale
  • _id (PK)
  • invoiceNo
  • lineItems[]
  • gstTotal, total
  • paymentMode
  • customerId (FK)
Customer
  • _id (PK)
  • name, phone
  • creditBalance
KhataEntry
  • _id (PK)
  • customerId (FK)
  • type (credit/pay)
  • amount, note
  • saleId (FK)
StockAdjustment
  • _id (PK)
  • productId (FK)
  • qty, reason
  • date
Supplier
  • _id (PK)
  • name, contact
  • products[]
Alert / Counter
  • _id (PK)
  • type, message
  • seq (invoice no.)

Relationships:  User 1—N Product, Sale, Customer, Supplier  •  Customer 1—N KhataEntry  •  Sale 1—1 KhataEntry (credit sale)  •  Product 1—N StockAdjustment  •  Counter provides atomic, gap-free invoice numbers. All money fields use Decimal128.
