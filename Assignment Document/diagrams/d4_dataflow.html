Core Business Data Flow
Add Stock
Products & opening stock entered (or imported via OCR / supplier)
→
Quick Sale
Search product, enter weight or amount, GST applied per item
→
Invoice
PDF invoice + UPI QR generated; stock auto-reduced
→
Payment / Khata
Cash / UPI recorded, or credit posted to customer ledger
→
AI Insights
History analysed for forecasts, reorder report & alerts
A single transaction updates stock, billing, payment and the customer ledger; accumulated data feeds the AI layer.
