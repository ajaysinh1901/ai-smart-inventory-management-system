System Module Diagram
AI Smart Inventory Management System

Authentication & Onboarding

Register / login with JWT, bcrypt-hashed passwords, guided 7-step onboarding wizard.

Inventory Management

Products, units of measure, stock levels, low-stock detection, shrinkage records.

Quick Sale & Billing

Amount/weight-first billing, GST computation, PDF invoice with UPI QR code.

Customers & Digital Khata

Customer directory and credit ledger with running balances and repayments.

Supplier Management

Supplier records, purchase transactions and sample-pack shrinkage reports.

AI Insights

Gemini-powered demand forecasting, reorder report and in-app help assistant.

OCR Bill Scanner

Tesseract.js extracts products from photographed supplier bills for import.

Analytics & Reports

KPI dashboard, sales trends, scheduled reports and Tally-compatible export.

Smart Alerts (Cron)

Scheduled jobs raise low-stock, expiry and anomaly notifications automatically.

Each module is implemented as an independent route group on the server with a matching set of client pages.
